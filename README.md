## About This Repository

This is a comprehensive digital walking-tour guide to the **Vatican Museums** (*Musei Vaticani*) in Vatican City. It documents every major room, gallery, and artwork along the standard visitor route — from the entrance at Viale Vaticano to the Sistine Chapel at the route's end. 

The companion website at **[NullLabTests.github.io/vatican-museums-tour](https://NullLabTests.github.io/vatican-museums-tour/)** provides an interactive tour with artwork images, descriptions, and a navigable museum map.

## The Tour

The Vatican Museums span **54 galleries across 22 collections**, with roughly **7 km of exhibition route** (the standard visitor path is ~4.8 km). The museums hold approximately **70,000 works** in total, with about **20,000 on display**. In 2024 alone, **6.8 million visitors** passed through — making it the world's second most-visited art museum after the Louvre.

The route is designed as a one-way journey through centuries of art and history:

1. **Ancient World** → Egyptian, Etruscan, and Greco-Roman collections
2. **Classical Sculpture** → The Pio-Clementino masterpieces (Laocoön, Apollo Belvedere)
3. **Renaissance Splendor** → Raphael Rooms and the Sistine Chapel ceiling
4. **Baroque & Beyond** → Caravaggio, the Gallery of Maps, Modern Religious Art

| Collection | Period | Key Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| **Museo Gregoriano Egiziano** | Ancient Egypt | Mummies, "yellow coffins," Sekhmet statues, Book of the Dead |
| **Museo Pio-Clementino** | Classical Antiquity | Laocoön, Apollo Belvedere, Belvedere Torso, Sleeping Ariadne |
| **Museo Chiaramonti** | Roman Portraiture | ~1,000 busts, Augustus of Prima Porta (Braccio Nuovo) |
| **Museo Gregoriano Etrusco** | Etruscan Civilization | Regolini-Galassi tomb gold, Mars of Todi, Exekias amphora |
| **Museo Gregoriano Egiziano** | Ancient Egypt | Mummies, Sekhmet statues, colossal Nile statue |
| **Pinacoteca Vaticana** | 12th–19th Century Painting | Giotto, Leonardo, Raphael, Caravaggio |
| **Raphael Rooms** | High Renaissance | School of Athens, Disputation of the Holy Sacrament |
| **Sistine Chapel** | High Renaissance | Michelangelo's ceiling and Last Judgment |
| **Gallery of Maps** | 16th Century Cartography | 40 frescoed maps of Italy, 120m corridor |
| **Borgia Apartment** | 15th Century | Pinturicchio frescoes; Modern Art (Van Gogh, Matisse, Chagall) |
