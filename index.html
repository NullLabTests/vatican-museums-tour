Essential Tips

  • Book online to skip the line (tickets.museivaticani.va)
  • Arrive early — crowds peak after 10am
  • No flash photography; Sistine Chapel is strict
  • Silence enforced in the Sistine Chapel
  • Allow 3–4 hours minimum for the full route
  • The Sistine Chapel is the last room — no side exit

Free Sundays 2026

  • Jan 25, Feb 22, Mar 29, Apr 26
  • May 31, Jun 28, Jul 26, Aug 30
  • Sep 27, Oct 25, Nov 29, Dec 27
  • Free entry 09:00–14:00 (last entry 12:30)
  • No online booking for free Sundays

Facilities

  • Cloakroom and luggage storage
  • Cafeteria and bistrot
  • Bookshop and Vatican Post Office
  • First aid station
  • Elevators and wheelchair ramps
  • Audio guides available (+€8)

Getting There

  • Metro: Ottaviano (Line A)
  • Bus: 40, 64, 49, 62, 23
  • Address: Viale Vaticano 6, 00192 Roma
  • Security check required at entrance
  • No large bags or liquids allowed
