# Raphael Rooms (Stanze di Raffaello)

Four rooms frescoed by Raphael and his workshop for Julius II and Leo X (1508-1524).

## Stanza della Segnatura (1508-1511)
Julius II's private library. Frescoes: Disputation of the Holy Sacrament (West), School of Athens (East), Parnassus (North), Cardinal Virtues (South).

## Stanza di Eliodoro (1511-1514)
Audience room. Frescoes: Expulsion of Heliodorus (West), Mass at Bolsena (East), Liberation of St. Peter (North), Meeting of Leo the Great and Attila (South).

## Stanza dell'Incendio del Borgo (1514-1517)
Music room for Leo X. Frescoes: Fire in the Borgo (West), Battle of Ostia (East), Coronation of Charlemagne (South), Oath of Leo III (North).

## Sala di Costantino (1517-1524)
Largest room, painted entirely after Raphael's death by his workshop. Frescoes: Vision of the Cross, Battle of Milvian Bridge, Baptism of Constantine, Donation of Constantine.
