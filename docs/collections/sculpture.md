# Sculpture Collections

## Museo Chiaramonti
~1,000 Roman portrait busts in a long loggia. Founded by Pius VII Chiaramonti.

## Braccio Nuovo
68m neoclassical gallery. Augustus of Prima Porta, Doryphoros copy, Nile statue, Gilded Bronze Peacocks.

## Gregorian Etruscan Museum
22 rooms. Regolini-Galassi tomb assemblage, Mars of Todi, Greek vase collection (Exekias amphora).

## Gregorian Egyptian Museum
9 rooms + hemicycle. Mummies, Sekhmet statues, Osiris-Antinous, colossal Nile, bronze Pinecone.
