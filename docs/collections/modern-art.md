# Borgia Apartment & Modern Art

## Borgia Apartment (Pinturicchio, 1492-1494)
Pope Alexander VI's private apartments. Six rooms: Pontiffs, Liberal Arts, Saints, Mysteries, Sibyls, Creed.

## Modern & Contemporary Art Collection
Inaugurated 1973 by Paul VI. ~8,000 works by ~250 artists in 55 rooms.

Key artists: Van Gogh (Pieta), Matisse (Vence Chapel drawings), Chagall, Bacon, Dali, de Chirico, Picasso, Klee, Gauguin, Burri, Fontana, Morandi, Manzu, Guttuso.
