# Pinacoteca Vaticana

Inaugurated 1932, designed by Luca Beltrami. ~460 paintings in 18 chronological rooms.

## Key Works
- Room II: Giotto, Stefaneschi Triptych (c. 1320)
- Room III: Fra Angelico, Stories of St. Nicholas (1437)
- Room IV: Melozzo da Forli, Sixtus IV appoints Platina (1477)
- Room VIII: Raphael, Transfiguration (1516-1520), Madonna of Foligno
- Room IX: Leonardo da Vinci, St. Jerome in the Wilderness (1480-1482)
- Room XII: Caravaggio, Deposition (1603-1604), Poussin, Martyrdom of St Erasmus
- Room XIII: Bernini, models for Cathedra Petri
