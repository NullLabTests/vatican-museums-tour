# The Three Great Galleries

## Galleria dei Candelabri
~80m corridor, six sections. Ancient marble candelabra, statues (Hypnos, Thanatos, Old Fisherman), Ludwig Seitz ceiling frescoes honoring St. Thomas Aquinas.

## Galleria degli Arazzi
~75m corridor. Raphael tapestries (woven in Brussels by Pieter van Aelst, 1516-1521), ancient pontifical tapestries, trompe-l'oeil floor.

## Galleria delle Carte Geografiche
120m corridor. 40 frescoed maps of Italy (1580-1581) coordinated by Egnazio Danti. 51 ceiling panels. The world's largest pictorial geographical study.
