# Sistine Chapel

Named for Pope Sixtus IV who restored the Cappella Magna 1477-1480. Consecrated 15 August 1483.

## Michelangelo's Ceiling (1508-1512)
Nine central panels depicting Genesis: Separation of Light from Darkness, Creation of Sun/Moon/Plants, Separation of Land and Water, Creation of Adam, Creation of Eve, Fall and Expulsion, Sacrifice of Noah, Great Flood, Drunkenness of Noah. Surrounded by 12 Prophets/Sibyls, 20 ignudi, 4 pendentives, 10 medallions, and Ancestors of Christ in lunettes.

## Wall Frescoes (1481-1482)
Life of Christ (North Wall) and Life of Moses (South Wall) by Perugino, Botticelli, Ghirlandaio, Rosselli, and Signorelli.

## The Last Judgment (1536-1541)
Michelangelo's altar wall fresco: Christ in glory, the saved rising, the damned dragged to hell. Charon and Minos from Dante. Michelangelo's self-portrait on the flayed skin of St. Bartholomew.

## Rules
Absolute silence enforced. Photography strictly forbidden.
