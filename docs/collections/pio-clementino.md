# Museo Pio-Clementino

Founded 1771 by Clement XIV, enlarged by Pius VI. The first public museum of antiquity in the modern sense.

## Key Galleries

- **Octagonal Court**: Laocoon and His Sons, Apollo Belvedere
- **Sala Rotonda**: Gilded Bronze Hercules, Braschi Antinous
- **Greek Cross Gallery**: Porphyry Sarcophagi of Helena and Constance
- **Gallery of the Statues**: Sleeping Ariadne, Barberini Candelabra
- **Cabinet of the Masks**: Three Graces, theater mask floor mosaic
- **Sala delle Muse**: Belvedere Torso (centerpiece)
- **Sala degli Animali**: Ancient animal statues, river-god figures
