## Essential Tips

- **Book online** to skip the line — on-site queues can exceed 2 hours
- **Arrive early** — crowds peak after 10am; the first hour is significantly quieter
- **Photography** — permitted (no flash) in the museums; strictly forbidden in the Sistine Chapel
- **Silence** — enforced in the Sistine Chapel by guards
- **Allow 3–4 hours** minimum for the full route
- The **Sistine Chapel is the last room** — there is no side exit
- **Dress code** — shoulders and knees must be covered (it's a consecrated space)
- **Metro:** Ottaviano (Line A) is the closest station
